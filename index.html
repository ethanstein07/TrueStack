Full app prototype · two phones, one app

One app on every phone. Host a game or join one.

Maya and Jordan have the same app. Either one can host with their TrueStack set, and the other can join. The steps below follow Maya hosting, but you can tap "Host a game" on Jordan's phone instead. Sam and Priya join from their own phones, so you'll see them show up.

How the set and the app work together

  1. Bluetooth. Phones near a set find its open game. A QR code or a 4-letter code works too.
  2. Pay, then unlock. Buy-ins go into the game pot, held by a licensed payment partner. Only then does the tray open and the chips you take unlock to you. Chips nobody paid for stay locked and are worth nothing at cash-out.
  3. RFID in every chip. A reader under each column counts chips leaving and coming back. Chips from another set don't count.
  4. Exact payouts. Cash-outs pay what the tray counted, straight to the player's TrueStack wallet.
  5. Fees. Debit cards are always free, for buy-ins and withdrawals. Credit cards always add the card fee (2.9% + 30¢), shown before paying.
